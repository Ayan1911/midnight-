# Midnight VoteZK — Video Recording Guide

To prove to the Midnight Network Mentors that your application relies entirely on physical ZK proofs and real blockchain execution rather than simulations, follow this strict recording script.

## Preparation Before Recording

1. **Environment Setup:** Ensure your `.env` contains `DEPLOYER_MNEMONIC` and points to the Midnight Preview testnet.
2. **Lace Wallet Beta:** Ensure you have the Midnight Lace Beta extension installed in your Chromium browser and unlocked.
3. **Indexer Active:** Verify the [Midnight Explorer](https://explorer.preview.midnight.network) is loading.

---

## 🎬 Video Recording Script (approx. 2-3 mins)

### Step 1: On-Chain Deployment Proof
1. Start screen recording.
2. Open your VS Code terminal and show `package.json` to prove dependencies (`@midnight-ntwrk/midnight-js-contracts`) are installed.
3. Run `npm run deploy:preview`.
4. Leave the terminal open showing the real `Contract Address` and `Transaction Hash` generated by the script. 
5. Point out the line where it says "Confirmed at Block Height".

### Step 2: Strict Lace Verification (No Mocks)
1. In your browser, open the developer console (F12).
2. Type `window.midnight.mnLace` and hit enter. Show the camera that the object exists.
3. Run `npm run dev` in a second terminal tab and open `http://localhost:5173`.
4. Click the "Connect Lace Wallet" button in the navigation bar.
5. **CRITICAL:** Show the DApp Connector popup appearing from the Lace Extension. Click "Authorize". 
6. Show that the wallet address in the top right correctly truncates your real Lace address.

### Step 3: Zero-Knowledge Proving & Execution
1. Scroll down to the Voting Station.
2. Click "Generate 32-Byte Secret" (Witness generation).
3. Select "Candidate Alpha".
4. Click "Submit ZK Proof to Ledger".
5. **CRITICAL:** Show the Lace Wallet transaction popup appearing to ask you to sign the ZK proof generation and pay the `tDUST` fee.
6. Click "Sign" / "Confirm" in Lace.
7. Show the real-time terminal/UI logs displaying "ZK-SNARK proof synthesized successfully" and wait for the "Transaction confirmed via Lace Wallet" notification.

### Step 4: Explorer Verification
1. Copy the `TxHash` from the success notification.
2. Open [https://explorer.preview.midnight.network](https://explorer.preview.midnight.network).
3. Paste the transaction hash into the search bar.
4. Show the confirmed transaction on the public block explorer, proving that no user identity or choice is visible in the transaction payload.

*Stop Recording. Upload to Loom or YouTube and include the link in your README.*
